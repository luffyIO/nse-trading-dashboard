How the agent learns and manages risk ＋

The agent waits for at least 240 completed bars, compares a bounded EMA parameter set across three chronological walk-forward windows, and tests the selected parameters once on the final holdout.

Learning
Uses up to 1,200 completed bars and a fixed EMA candidate set. Candidates must trade in at least two of three chronological windows; the score rewards return while penalizing drawdown.
Holdout
The final 20% is excluded from parameter selection. At least three holdout trades, positive net return, drawdown at or below 15%, and outperformance of a cost-adjusted buy-and-hold benchmark are required before considering a paper setup.
Decision
No rule has been trained yet.
Persistence
Completed market bars are stored only in this browser's local storage, capped at 1,200 bars per symbol.
Safety
This is an educational paper-only signal. It never submits orders, connects to a broker, or guarantees performance. Backtests use close-only data and estimated costs.

Inspired by the observe/train/evaluate/repeat concept in Lewis Jackson Investing's video. The video transcript was unavailable during implementation; this agent is an independently implemented, explainable adaptation, not a reproduction of the video's code.

Backtest assumptions & risk disclosure
How to read these results: Backtests use only the currently available 5-minute close series for the selected symbol. Signals are evaluated on a completed bar and filled at the next available close; an illustrative 0.10% cost is applied on each entry and exit, with no slippage, taxes, liquidity, corporate actions, or market-impact model. The current feed provides limited intraday history, not a robust multi-year test. Few or zero trades mean the displayed metrics are not statistically meaningful. Simulations are hypothetical, may be affected by data gaps and overfitting, and do not guarantee future performance. No strategy can assure profits; this dashboard does not place trades or provide personalized financial advice.
